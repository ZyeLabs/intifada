About the event

Social Intifada has officially launched its new website, marking a significant step in expanding its digital presence. The platform aims to provide news, analysis, and perspectives on social and political issues from around the world.

The website is designed to serve as a central hub for content, offering readers accessible coverage, opinion pieces, and updates on current events. Organizers say the launch reflects a broader effort to strengthen engagement and reach a wider audience online.

With its debut, Social Intifada joins a growing number of independent platforms seeking to shape conversations and provide alternative viewpoints in the digital media space.
