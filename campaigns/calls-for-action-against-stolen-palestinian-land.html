About the campaign

A campaign is calling on people to take action against a virtual real estate expo advertising stolen Palestinian land.
The online event, set for Sunday, April 19, is hosted by an Israeli real estate agency and targets international buyers. Activists say these land sales continue despite ongoing violence and displacement in Palestine.
Supporters are urged to join the virtual expo and disrupt it, raising awareness and putting pressure on organizers. Campaign materials say, “The more people who join, the harder it will be for them to sell stolen land.”
Organizers describe this as a way to challenge injustice and stand against the ongoing situation in Palestine.
The campaign is spreading online, calling for global participation and action.
