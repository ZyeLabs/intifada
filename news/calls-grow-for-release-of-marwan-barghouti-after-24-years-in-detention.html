Calls Grow for Release of Marwan Barghouti After 24 Years in Detention

Marwan Barghouti has been imprisoned by Israel since 2002. Supporters are calling for his release and allege abuse in custody, though this is unverified.

By Editorial • April 15, 2026

Marwan Barghouti, a prominent Palestinian political figure, has been imprisoned by Israel since April 2002.
As he marks 24 years in detention, supporters and advocacy groups are calling for his release and drawing renewed international attention to his case.
Recent claims from family members and supporters allege he was subjected to abuse in custody, including assaults. These allegations have not been independently verified.
Human rights groups have also raised broader concerns about conditions for Palestinian detainees and called for greater transparency and accountability.
Barghouti was convicted by an Israeli court for involvement in attacks during the Second Intifada, a point cited by critics who support his continued imprisonment.
His case remains a focal point of international debate over justice, human rights, and the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.
