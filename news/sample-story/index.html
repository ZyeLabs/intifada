Community briefing: why digital pressure still matters

A static article template for the new branch, designed for direct edits in the repository.

By Editorial Desk - April 25, 2026

This starter story exists to prove the static article route, typography, image treatment, share controls, and related-card layout. Replace it with real editorial copy when the branch becomes the publishing source.

Static pages make deployment simple: every public page is an HTML file, every image lives in the repository, and GitHub Pages can serve the branch directly from the root.

The tradeoff is editorial workflow. New stories, tags, dates, and archive entries must be updated manually across the relevant HTML files.
